WHAT YOU ARE WATCHING

Probabilistic policy inside a hard safety envelope

The road event is sampled from P(event | state). Unsafe actor actions are filtered before System One sees them, then the sampled action is executed through a 12 Hz control-barrier shield. The shield may override the requested acceleration to preserve the configured minimum gap.

CONTROL OBJECTIVE

Hard gap constraint, soft 80 mph cruise target

For safe-cruise runs, following distance is an invariant enforced by the runtime. Cruise speed and comfortable following distance are soft objectives expressed to the probabilistic controller; the page exposes when the runtime shield had to intervene.
